Your privacy is important to us. KhataBanao is designed to help you manage customer records, udhaar, payments, and business transactions.

1. Introduction

Welcome to KhataBanao. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application or website.

By using KhataBanao, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information You Enter

KhataBanao allows users to enter and manage information such as:

  • Customer names
  • Customer phone numbers
  • Udhaar and payment records
  • Transaction amounts and dates
  • Business notes
  • Application preferences

This information is entered by users to maintain their business records.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into KhataBanao is used within the app to:

  • Maintain customer ledgers
  • Calculate outstanding balances
  • Record payments and udhaar
  • Display transaction histories
  • Generate statements and reports
  • Export business records

4. Local Data Storage

The current KhataBanao web application stores customer and transaction records locally using browser or Android WebView storage on your device.

The provided application does not require user accounts and does not provide automatic cloud synchronization.

Customer ledger records are not automatically uploaded to a developer database by the current web application.

5. Data Sharing

The current KhataBanao web application does not automatically send customer ledger records to advertisers or analytics providers.

If you export, download, print, or share a backup or customer statement, you are responsible for where that information is stored or shared.

6. Third-Party Services

The provided web application does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics scripts, or online payment processing services.

However, website hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs when the website is visited.

If future versions include additional third-party services, this policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Data Security

We encourage users to protect their devices and business records.

Local browser storage is not an encrypted or password-protected vault. Exported backup files may also contain readable customer information.

Please avoid sharing sensitive business records with unauthorized individuals.

8. Data Backup

KhataBanao provides options to export business records in supported formats, including JSON and CSV.

Users are responsible for protecting their downloaded backup files.

Backups saved outside the app must be deleted separately when they are no longer needed.

9. Data Deletion

Users can manage and remove locally stored records from within the application.

To remove all ledger records, open the application Settings and select the option to delete all records.

You may also clear the website's stored data through your browser or device settings.

Deleted local records cannot be recovered by the developer. Previously exported backup files are not automatically deleted.

10. Children's Privacy

KhataBanao is intended for general business and bookkeeping purposes and is not specifically designed for children.

Users should not enter children's personal information without an appropriate legal basis.

11. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful reason to record and manage customer information.

Users should protect customer privacy and avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
